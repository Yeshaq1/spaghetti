Frequently asked questions

Can I really work from anywhere?

Yes. We only ask for a few hours each day that overlap with the client you’re working with.

How much time do I need to give?

You choose for each project, from a few hours a week to full-time. You can change it whenever your life changes.

I know my industry better than I know AI, or the other way round. Should I apply?

Yes. People who have both are rare, and teams that mix them do the best work. Tell us honestly where you’re strongest.

What happens to my information?

We use it only to match you with projects. We never sell it or share it with a client without asking you. Email info@spgti.com to update or delete it at any time.

Untangle Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026 · Untangle is made by Spaghetti

Untangle is a Chrome extension that reads the LinkedIn posts on your screen and checks them against criteria you set (your "lenses" and the built-in slop check), using TypeSafe's Jev model. This policy explains what data the extension handles and where it goes.

In short: Spaghetti runs no servers for Untangle and receives none of your data. Post text is sent directly from your browser to TypeSafe for analysis, using your own API key. Everything else stays in your browser.

What the extension processes

Data Why Where it goes
Text of LinkedIn posts that scroll into view, and the author's name and headline shown on the post To answer your lenses and the slop check Sent to TypeSafe's API (api.typesafe.ai) for analysis
Your "About you" description and your lens questions Gives Jev context to answer your lenses Sent to TypeSafe with each analysis request; stored in Chrome's extension storage
Your TypeSafe API key To authenticate requests to TypeSafe Stored in Chrome's extension storage; sent only to TypeSafe
Analysis results To avoid re-checking posts you've already seen Cached in your browser's session storage, cleared when the browser closes
Usage counters (posts checked, tokens used) To show your stats and Jev spend in the popup Stored locally in your browser only

Settings (your lenses, "About you", and API key) are saved with Chrome's storage.sync. If you have Chrome sync turned on, Google syncs them across your signed-in browsers, as with any extension setting.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, store, or see any of your data. Untangle has no Spaghetti backend, analytics, or tracking.
  • We don't read pages other than linkedin.com, and on LinkedIn we only read posts as they appear on your screen. We don't read messages, and we don't automate scrolling or visit profiles.
  • We don't sell or share data, use it for advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

TypeSafe

Analysis is performed by TypeSafe (typesafe.ai) under your own TypeSafe account. TypeSafe states that Jev is not trained on customer requests or responses. Its handling of the data you send is governed by TypeSafe's legal terms and privacy policy.

Limited Use

Untangle's use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the extension's single purpose: analyzing the LinkedIn posts you view against criteria you define.

Your control

  • Pause Untangle at any time with the switch in the popup.
  • Remove your API key, lenses, and "About you" in the settings page, or uninstall the extension to delete all locally stored data.

Children

Untangle is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and, for material changes, note it in the extension's store listing.

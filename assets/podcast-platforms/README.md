# Podcast platform artwork

Downloaded on 2026-11-06. These SVGs are vendor-supplied artwork, kept unchanged.

- `apple-podcasts-listen.svg`: `Apple_Podcast_Listen_on_Badge_RGB_USGB-EN_CI_090126.svg` from the [asset folder linked by Apple](https://apple.box.com/s/ov4uvkt6lk3fv2fdj2i6p8iwh3ljc0c3).
- `spotify-logo-white.svg`: `Full_Logo_White_RGB.svg` from [Spotify's official logo download](https://developer.spotify.com/images/guidelines/design/2024-spotify-full-logo.zip).

Guidelines: [Apple Podcasts](https://marketing.services.apple/apple-podcasts-identity-guidelines), [Spotify](https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design).

Use Apple's complete badge first in the row, with its original proportions, border, wording, and at least 10% of its height as surrounding clear space. Do not shorten the service name or recreate its artwork. The badge is displayed above Apple's 30px minimum height.

The Spotify link contains the official full white logo on black. Its 90px width exceeds the 70px minimum, and the link reserves more than half the icon's height around the logo. The surrounding link is site CSS, not a Spotify-supplied badge. Do not redraw, recolor, distort, or add effects to either asset.

The two links have equal dimensions; the artwork keeps its own proportions. Do not stretch artwork to force equal logo dimensions.
